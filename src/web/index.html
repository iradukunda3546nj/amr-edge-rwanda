    Disclaimer
    
        
    We trained this digit recogniser on the public meter-wheel dataset of Nikić, Bortnik, Lukić, Vukobratović and Mezei, "Lightweight Digit Recognition in Smart Metering System Using Narrowband Internet of Things and Federated Learning", Future Internet 16(11):402, 2024 (GPL-3.0), together with synthetic register images we generated. That dataset comes from a single meter model. Accuracy on other meters therefore depends on image quality and register layout, and an operator may need to mark the register region. The cellular session, the WASAC back end, the customer accounts, the tariff and the *150# short code shown on this page are simulated or illustrative. They do not represent WASAC systems or data.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    Path to deployment
    
        
    For field deployment, we intend to fine-tune the model on images collected from the meter types WASAC has installed in Rwanda, and to validate it against manual readings in a supervised pilot across Gasabo, Kicukiro and Nyarugenge. We also intend to measure on-device latency and battery life on production hardware, and to integrate with WASAC billing and an approved SMS short code, in coordination with WASAC and RURA.
